Frequently asked questions

Open Upload from the home page. Choose the type (Music, Photos, Videos, Apps, Software or DJ Mixes), fill in the form, select your files, then tap Upload. Keep the page open until it finishes.
After a successful upload, go back to the main menu (Home) and refresh the page. If you stay on the upload page without refreshing Home, new content may not appear yet.
Open the item in the library, then tap Download. A short wait screen may appear first; when it finishes, the download starts. Do not close the page during that wait.
When uploading, add a cover image (and profile photo if asked). If you skipped them, the item may show a blank or default icon. You can upload again with the correct images.
Browsing and downloading follow the rules of Ning Wei Media. Always respect artists and copyright. Do not upload content you do not have the right to share.
Note the file name and the folder it is in. When Contact channels are fully opened later, you will be able to report from there. For now, use the Contact page flow if needed.
Open the Contact page. Email and WhatsApp are protected: tapping them starts a short ad. Personal details are not published on the site yet for safety.
